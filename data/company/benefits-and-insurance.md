# Employee Benefits and Insurance

## Overview
All full-time employees at Acme Corp receive comprehensive benefits package starting from their first day. This includes health insurance, life cover, wellness allowance, learning budget, and provident fund contributions.

## Health Insurance
Employees are entitled to ₹5 lakh family floater health insurance policy from day one of employment. This covers the employee, spouse, children, and parents. Parents can opt for additional top-up coverage at their own expense. Claims process is managed through our insurance provider with 24/7 support.

## Life Insurance
The company provides term life cover equal to three times the employee's annual salary. This policy ensures financial security for dependents in case of unforeseen circumstances. The policy is managed by our corporate insurance partner.

## Wellness and Learning Benefits
Each employee receives ₹12,000 annually for wellness activities including gym memberships, health checkups, or yoga classes. Additionally, there is a learning budget of ₹30,000 per year for professional development courses, certifications, or conference attendance.

## Provident Fund
The company contributes 12% of basic salary towards the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF). This contribution is automatically deducted from monthly salary and matched by the employer. The fund accumulates interest at prevailing rates.

## Enrollment Process
New hires are automatically enrolled in all benefits upon joining. For any changes or queries, contact HR department at hr@acme.example. Insurance enrollment requires submission of medical documents and family details within 30 days of joining.
