# Business Travel Policy

## Overview
This policy outlines guidelines for business travel at Acme Corp. All travel must be pre-approved and booked through the designated travel desk to ensure compliance and cost optimization.

## Booking Requirements
All business travel must be booked through the company's travel desk at least 7 days in advance of the travel date. This allows sufficient time for approval processes and ensures availability of preferred accommodations and transportation options.

## Travel Class and Duration
- **Flight Class**: Economy class is required for all domestic trips under 6 hours duration
- **Per Diem**: ₹1,500 per day for domestic business travel
- **Hotel Budget**: 
  - ₹6,000 per night maximum in metro cities (Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad)
  - ₹4,000 per night maximum in non-metro locations

## Approval Process
- **Domestic Trips**: Require manager approval before booking
- **International Trips**: Require VP-level approval before booking

## Cancellation Policy
- Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours prior to departure to avoid penalties
- Any cancellation within 24 hours of booking will result in full forfeiture of the travel cost
- Rebooking fees and change charges are the responsibility of the traveler

## Additional Notes
Travel expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 15 days of return. All receipts must be attached to expense reports for reimbursement approval.
