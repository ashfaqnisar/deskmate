# Leave Policy

## Annual (Earned) Leave
Full-time employees receive **18 days** of annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per month.
Up to **10 unused days** may be carried forward to the next year. Carried-forward days expire on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive **8 days** of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than **2 consecutive days**.

## Casual Leave
Employees receive **6 days** of casual leave per year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot exceed 3 consecutive days.

## Applying for Leave
- Annual leave must be requested at least **7 days in advance** for absences of 3+ days.
- All leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager.
- If the manager is unavailable for 3 working days, HR may approve instead.

## Parental Leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.
