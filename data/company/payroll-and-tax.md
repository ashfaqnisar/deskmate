# Payroll and Tax Guide

## Salary Payment Schedule
Salaries are processed and paid on the last working day of each month. This ensures consistent payment timing regardless of weekends or holidays. Employees receive their salary credits directly into their bank accounts by this date.

## Payslip Distribution
Payslips are made available on the HR portal by the 3rd of each month. All employees must access their payslips through the secure HR portal to verify details. Any discrepancies in payslips should be reported immediately to the HR team.

## Reimbursement Processing
Reimbursement claims approved before the 20th of each month will be included in that month's payroll payment. This includes travel, meal, and other business-related expenses. Claims submitted after the 20th date will be processed in the following month's payroll cycle.

## Tax Filing Deadlines
Employees must submit their tax declaration forms by 15 January each year. Supporting documents and proof of investments must be submitted by 31 March. The company will issue Form 16 by 15 June for the previous financial year, which is essential for individual tax filings.

## Payroll Queries
For any payroll-related questions or concerns, employees should contact the HR department at hr@acme.example. The HR team handles all payroll inquiries and ensures timely resolution of issues.

## Important Notes
- Salary revisions occur annually in April
- Out-of-band salary increases require CHRO approval
- All tax-related documents must be submitted within specified deadlines to avoid penalties
- Employees should maintain records of their tax filings for audit purposes
