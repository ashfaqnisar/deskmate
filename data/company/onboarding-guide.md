# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

## Day One Setup
On your first day, you will receive a company laptop from IT. Your Okta account will be created and sent to your work email. Complete MFA setup using Okta Verify app on your mobile device. Configure GlobalProtect VPN for remote access. A buddy will be assigned to help with initial questions and orientation.

## IT Infrastructure Access
All IT issues must be logged in Jira under the IT project. Use ticket type "Software Request" for software installations. For technical problems, create a P2 high priority ticket. VPN access requires successful MFA authentication before connecting.

## Training Requirements
Complete mandatory security and compliance training within 14 days of joining. This includes data protection, acceptable use policies, and company-specific security protocols. The training will be scheduled by your manager and HR department.

## Probation Period
Your probation period is 6 months with performance review at 3 months. A 30-60-90 day plan must be agreed upon with your manager outlining goals and expectations for each phase of your onboarding.

## Leave and Work from Home Policy
You will accrue 18 days annual leave (carry forward up to 10 days, expiring 31 March), 8 sick leave, and 6 casual leave days. Work from home is allowed up to 2 days per week with mandatory in-office presence on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Monthly internet allowance of ₹1,500 is available through the expense portal.

## Equipment Replacement
Company laptops are replaced every 3 years. Software requests must go through Jira IT ticketing system for approval and installation.
