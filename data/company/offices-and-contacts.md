# Offices and Key Contacts

## Office Locations and Roles

Acme Corp operates three primary offices in India: Bengaluru (Headquarters), Pune, and Hyderabad. The Bengaluru office serves as the central hub for strategic planning and executive functions. Pune handles backend development and quality assurance operations. Hyderabad focuses on cloud infrastructure and DevOps support. All offices maintain standard business hours from 10:00 to 18:00 IST.

## Escalation Path

For issues requiring escalation: Employee → Manager → HR → CHRO. This ensures proper oversight and resolution within established company protocols.

## Additional Services

All offices feature subsidised cafeteria services with daily meal options available at reduced rates. Visitor policy requires all guests to register at the reception desk before entering office premises, maintaining security standards and tracking access.

## Work from Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to two days per week, with mandatory in-office presence on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Internet allowance of ₹1,500 per month is available through the expense portal for remote workers.
