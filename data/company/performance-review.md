# Performance Review Process

## Overview
Acme Corp conducts annual performance reviews to evaluate employee contributions and plan career development. Reviews follow a structured timeline with key milestones in April (goals), October (mid-year check-in), and March (annual review). All employees participate in the process, which directly impacts salary revisions, promotions, and professional growth.

## Review Timeline
- **April**: Goal setting for the review period begins
- **October**: Mid-year check-in conducted with managers
- **March**: Annual performance review completed

## Rating System
Performance ratings use a 1 to 5 scale where:
- 1 = Unsatisfactory
- 2 = Needs Improvement  
- 3 = Meets Expectations (baseline)
- 4 = Exceeds Expectations
- 5 = Outstanding

## Salary and Promotion Impact
Rating results directly influence April salary revisions and promotion decisions. Employees must achieve a minimum rating of 3 to qualify for standard salary increases. Higher ratings may lead to promotions or special recognition.

## Underperformance Protocol
Employees rated below 3 who cannot improve within 60 days will receive a formal improvement plan. This plan includes specific milestones, support mechanisms, and a clear timeline for review.

## Self-Review and Feedback
All employees may submit self-reviews during the process. Managers must provide feedback to employees before final rating completion. Employees can request additional feedback sessions from their managers at any time during the review cycle.
