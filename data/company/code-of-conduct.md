# Code of Conduct

## Respectful Workplace
Acme Corp maintains a professional environment where all employees treat colleagues with dignity. Harassment, bullying, or discriminatory behavior in any form is strictly prohibited. Employees must communicate respectfully, regardless of role, hierarchy, or personal differences. Disputes should be addressed through direct conversation or manager escalation rather than public confrontation.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must avoid situations where personal interests could conflict with company objectives. This includes refraining from conducting business with Acme Corp vendors, investing in competitors, or using company resources for personal gain. Any potential conflicts must be disclosed to your immediate manager within 10 working days.

## Gifts and Entertainment
Employees may accept gifts up to ₹5,000 in value from clients, vendors, or partners. All gifts exceeding this threshold must be declared to your manager and reported to HR via email at hr@acme.example. Personal entertainment expenses related to business meetings are limited to ₹2,000 per occasion. Gifts received during company events are exempt from reporting requirements.

## Harassment and Discrimination
Zero tolerance policy applies to all forms of harassment including sexual, racial, religious, or gender-based discrimination. This includes unwelcome conduct, offensive comments, or inappropriate physical contact. Any incident should be reported immediately to HR regardless of the perpetrator's position level.

## Reporting Concerns
All workplace concerns can be reported confidentially to HR at hr@acme.example. Employees may also contact the Security team at security@acme.example for serious incidents. Retaliation against anyone who reports issues in good faith is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.

## Use of Company Resources
Company laptops, software, internet, and equipment must be used solely for business purposes. Personal use is permitted within reason but not exceeding 30 minutes per workday. Employees must follow IT security protocols including MFA with Okta Verify and password policies requiring minimum 14 characters.
