# Manager Handbook

## Leave Management
Managers must approve employee leave requests within 2 working days of submission. Annual leave entitlement is 18 days with carry forward up to 10 days, expiring 31 March. Sick leave is 8 days and casual leave is 6 days annually. Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday mandatory in-office days.

## Expense Claims
Approve expense claims up to ₹25,000 per claim directly. Claims exceeding this amount must be escalated to the Vice President for approval. Work from home internet allowance is up to ₹1,500 per month via the expense portal.

## Hiring and Team Management
All hiring requires an approved headcount form before requisition. Conduct weekly one-on-one meetings with each direct report to discuss progress, concerns, and development goals. Address performance concerns immediately through documented conversations and performance improvement plans when necessary.

## Confidentiality Requirements
Team salary information and sensitive data are strictly confidential. Do not share salary details or personal data with unauthorized personnel. All HR and IT policies apply equally to all employees regardless of position level.

## Reporting and Escalation
Report any harassment complaints to HR within 24 hours of receipt. Maintain clear documentation of all employee interactions, performance discussions, and disciplinary actions. Ensure compliance with company policies including IT security protocols, password requirements (minimum 14 characters), MFA usage via Okta Verify, and VPN access through GlobalProtect.

## Salary and Compensation
Salary revisions occur annually in April. Out-of-band salary increases require approval from the Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO). Maintain accurate records of all compensation changes and communicate updates clearly to employees.
