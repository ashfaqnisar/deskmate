# IT Support Guide

## Raising a Ticket
All IT issues are tracked in Jira project **IT**. Priorities:
- **P1 (Critical):** company-wide outage or security incident. Response within 30 minutes.
- **P2 (High):** a single user fully blocked (e.g., laptop won't boot). Response within 4 hours.
- **P3 (Normal):** degraded but working (e.g., slow VPN). Response within 2 business days.

## Laptops
Laptops are replaced every **3 years**. Hardware faults go to IT as P2. Never take a company laptop to a third-party repair shop.

## Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated only when compromised. MFA via the Okta Verify app is mandatory.
IT will **never** ask for your password over chat or email.

## VPN
Use the GlobalProtect VPN when accessing internal systems from outside the office. VPN issues are P3 unless you are fully blocked.

## Software Requests
Request new software through a Jira IT ticket with type "Software Request". Licensed software needs manager approval.
