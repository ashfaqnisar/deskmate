# Compensation Review Process

## Overview
The annual compensation review cycle runs from February to April each year. This process ensures salary adjustments align with market standards, company performance, and individual contributions. All salary changes must remain within published pay bands as defined in the company's compensation framework.

## Merit Budget and Guidelines
The total merit budget for the year is set at 8% to 12% of total payroll. HR admins calculate and distribute this budget across all employees based on performance ratings. Salary adjustments are strictly maintained within established pay bands. Any exceptions requiring salary outside these bands require explicit approval from the Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO).

## Timeline and Calibration
Calibration meetings occur during the third week of March, where department heads review employee performance and make recommendations. The final review process concludes by 30 April, with all compensation changes implemented effective 1 May. All salary data remains confidential and is accessible only to HR administrators.

## Key Responsibilities
- Department managers conduct performance reviews and submit recommendations
- HR admins process final calculations and ensure compliance with pay bands
- CHRO approves any exceptions to standard compensation guidelines
- IT support assists with system updates for payroll processing

## Data Access Controls
Only HR administrators may view individual salary details. All compensation data is stored in encrypted systems with restricted access. Any unauthorized disclosure of salary information violates company security policies and may result in disciplinary action. The expense portal and Jira IT system maintain logs of all access to sensitive financial data.

## Approval Process
All salary adjustments require formal documentation in the HR portal. Changes exceeding standard guidelines must include justification and CHRO approval before implementation. This ensures consistency across departments while maintaining flexibility for exceptional cases.
