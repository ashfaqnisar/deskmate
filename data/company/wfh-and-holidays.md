# Work From Home & Holidays

## Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to **2 days per week**. Tuesdays and Thursdays are mandatory in-office "collab days".
Fully remote arrangements require VP approval.

## Internet Allowance
WFH employees receive a monthly internet reimbursement of up to **₹1,500**, claimed through the expense portal.

## Public Holidays 2026
Acme observes **12 public holidays** in 2026, including Republic Day (26 Jan), Holi, Independence Day (15 Aug), Gandhi Jayanti (2 Oct), Diwali (2 days), and Christmas (25 Dec).
Employees may also pick **2 optional holidays** from the regional list.
