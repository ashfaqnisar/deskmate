# Resignation and Exit Process

## Overview
This document outlines the resignation and exit process for all Acme Corp employees. The process ensures smooth transition of responsibilities and proper closure of employment ties.

## Notice Period
Employees must provide a minimum of 60 days written notice before resignation. During probation period (first 6 months), notice period is reduced to 30 days. Notice buy-out requests require approval from both the direct manager and HR department.

## Handover Requirements
Upon resignation, employees must prepare a detailed handover plan in consultation with their direct manager. This plan should cover:
- All ongoing projects and their current status
- Access credentials and system permissions
- Important contacts and stakeholders
- Documentation and knowledge transfer

## Exit Interview
All resigning employees must attend an exit interview with the HR department. The interview helps gather feedback on company policies, work environment, and potential improvements.

## Asset Return
On the last working day, employees must return:
- Company laptop and accessories
- Access cards and security badges
- Any company property or documentation
- Login credentials for personal accounts

## Leave Encashment
Unused annual leave will be encashed at the employee's daily rate. Sick and casual leaves are not encashed.

## Final Settlement
The final settlement, including salary arrears and encashment of leave, will be processed within 45 days of the last working day. Settlement will be issued via bank transfer to the employee's registered account.
