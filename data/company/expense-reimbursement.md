# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview
All business-related expenses incurred by Acme Corp employees must be submitted through the company's expense portal within 30 days of the transaction date. This policy ensures timely processing and proper documentation of all reimbursements.

## Submission Requirements
- Submit all claims via the expense portal within 30 days of spending
- Receipts are mandatory for all expenses exceeding ₹500
- Meal expenses while traveling are capped at ₹800 per day
- Internet allowance up to ₹1,500 per month is available for remote workers

## Approval Process
- Manager approval required before submission to finance
- Finance team processes approved claims within 7 working days
- Rejected claims may be resubmitted once with corrected documentation

## Eligible Expenses
- Business travel costs including accommodation and transportation
- Professional development materials and courses
- Work-related meals during business trips
- Internet allowance for remote work (₹1,500/month)
- Other expenses approved by manager and finance

## Important Notes
- All claims must include valid receipts and supporting documentation
- Expenses not submitted within 30 days may not be processed
- Unauthorized expenses will not be reimbursed
- Policy applies to all employees across Bengaluru, Pune, and Hyderabad offices
