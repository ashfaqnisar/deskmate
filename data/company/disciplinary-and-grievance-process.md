# Disciplinary and Grievance Process

## Overview
Acme Corp maintains a fair and structured disciplinary process for all employees. Grievances must be raised through HR within 30 days of the incident. All investigations are conducted by a committee formed within 5 working days of complaint receipt.

## Disciplinary Stages

### Verbal Warning
First offense for minor violations. HR issues verbal warning during team meeting or one-on-one session. Documented in employee file.

### Written Warning
Issued after second offense or serious misconduct. Employee receives formal written notice with specific violation details and improvement timeline (30 days).

### Final Warning
Given for repeated offenses or serious breaches. Includes detailed explanation of consequences if violations continue. Employee has 15 days to respond in writing.

### Termination
Occurs after final warning violation or severe misconduct. Process includes final meeting, written notice, and 30-day notice period.

## Investigation Process
- Committee formed within 5 working days of complaint
- Includes HR representative, immediate manager, and one peer employee
- Investigation completed within 10 working days
- All findings documented in formal report

## Grievance Procedure
Employees may raise grievances through:
- Direct communication with HR (hr@acme.example)
- Formal written letter to HR
- Jira ticket in IT project for system-related issues

## Appeals Process
- Appeal must be submitted to CHRO within 10 days of decision
- CHRO reviews case and makes final determination
- Appeal decision is final and binding
